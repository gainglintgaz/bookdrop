# compliance.md — Disclosure + Disclaimer Copy + Regulated-Domain Patterns

> **Authority:** Auto-loaded global rule (16th rule file). Cross-cutting across regulated verticals (fintech, bookkeeping, health, legal, financial advice).
> **Last updated:** 2026-05-14
> **Companion rules:** `privacy.md` (PII boundaries), `data-protection.md` (production data), `aggregate-design.md` (cohort/benchmark rules), `data-integrity.md` (DMG).
>
> **Standing rule:** I am not a lawyer or licensed financial advisor. This file provides DRAFT copy + patterns derived from real ship requirements. Every regulated product must have its specific disclaimers reviewed by an attorney + domain expert (CPA, MD, etc.) before public ship. AI-generated compliance copy is NEVER final.

---

## §1 — When this rule applies

This rule applies to any project surface where:

- A user could act on a number or recommendation in a way that affects their money, health, or legal standing
- The product touches taxes, financial advice, medical advice, legal advice, or fiduciary matters
- The product handles regulated data (SSN, EIN, bank, PHI, PII-at-scale, financial transactions)
- The product makes statements that could be construed as professional advice

If your project doesn't touch any of these, you don't need this rule. Skip.

## §2 — The 3-layer compliance copy stack

Every regulated surface needs three layers of disclosure copy, in order:

### Layer 1 — Inline disclaimer (next to the number)

Short, specific to the number/action. Visible at the moment of decision.

Examples:

```
Tax projection: $12,400 (estimate)
Based on 8 of ~24 expected paystubs.
NOT a substitute for filing. Discuss with your tax professional.
```

```
Suggested deduction: $4,200
This is a starting estimate. Verify with your CPA before claiming on your return.
```

```
Margin signal: -8% week-over-week
Informational only. Not investment advice.
```

### Layer 2 — Section header disclaimer (top of feature)

Slightly longer. Frames the whole feature.

```
## 2025 Tax Return

These numbers are estimates calculated from documents you've uploaded.
They are NOT a substitute for professional tax preparation.

Filing accuracy is your responsibility. Federal and state returns must
be reviewed by you (or your CPA) before submission. Numbers here may
be incomplete or change as you upload more documents.
```

### Layer 3 — Settings/Help full disclosure

Long-form. Covers liability + data handling + scope of advice. One per product, linked from every regulated surface.

Standard structure (adapt to product):

```markdown
## Disclaimers + Liability

### What this product is
[Product] is a tool for [specific scope]. It is informational only.

### What this product is NOT
- This product is NOT a substitute for professional advice (legal, financial, medical, tax, fiduciary)
- This product does NOT establish a professional relationship with any licensed advisor
- This product does NOT guarantee accuracy of estimates or projections
- This product is NOT regulated by [SEC / FINRA / IRS / etc.] as an advisor or broker

### Your responsibilities
- Verify all numbers against source documents before acting
- Consult a qualified professional before [filing taxes / making investment decisions / making medical decisions / signing legal documents]
- Notify us of any inaccurate output via [contact]

### Data handling
- We store [list of data types]
- We do NOT send [PII list] to AI services
- See Privacy Policy for full data handling disclosure

### AI / LLM disclosures
- Some outputs are generated by AI/LLM models
- AI outputs may contain errors ("hallucinations")
- All AI-generated numbers are flagged with confidence scores
- AI outputs are logged with `prompt_version` for audit
```

## §3 — Disclaimer copy templates (per domain)

### §3.1 — Tax / fintech

**Inline:**
```
Tax projection: $X (estimate based on N of M expected documents)
Estimate only. NOT for filing. Discuss with your tax professional.
```

**Section header:**
```
## [Tax Year] Federal Tax Return

This is a working estimate based on documents you've uploaded.
It is NOT your filed return. Federal and state taxes must be reviewed
by you (or your tax preparer) before submission.

Estimates change as you upload more documents. Numbers below assume
filing status: [Single / MFJ / MFS / HoH / QW].
```

**Settings full:**
```
## Tax Calculation Disclaimer

[Product] is a tax estimation tool. It is NOT a tax preparation service
or a substitute for a CPA. We are not a registered tax preparer (PTIN holder)
and we do not e-file returns on your behalf.

Tax law is complex and changes yearly. Our calculations are based on the
tax_year_constants table, updated as the IRS publishes new figures.
Federal law changes in mid-year may not be reflected immediately.

You are responsible for verifying every number on your filed return.
We strongly recommend a CPA review for any return with [self-employment
income / rental income / capital gains > $X / multi-state filings].
```

### §3.2 — Financial advice / wealth management

**Inline:**
```
Suggested savings rate: 18%
Educational only. Not investment advice. Past performance does not guarantee future results.
```

**Section header:**
```
## Portfolio Recommendation

These suggestions are educational and based on widely-accepted financial
principles (60/40 stock/bond, expense ratio under 0.5%, etc.). They are
NOT personalized investment advice.

We are not a registered investment advisor (RIA) or broker-dealer.
Consult a CFP or RIA before making any investment decision.
```

### §3.3 — Bookkeeping / CPA-facing

**Inline (every CPA-reportable number):**
```
P&L Net Income: $48,392 (computed from GL entries dated [start]-[end])
[Provenance: 412 GL entries · last updated 2026-05-13 14:23 · prompt_version 3.2]
```

**Section header:**
```
## [Client] Q2 2025 Financial Statements

Generated from documents and GL entries provided by [Client]. These
statements are working drafts subject to [Bookkeeper]'s review and
adjustment. They are NOT audited financial statements and have not
been reviewed under GAAP attest standards.

[Client] is responsible for the completeness and accuracy of source
documents. [Bookkeeper] is responsible for categorization accuracy
and final review.
```

### §3.4 — Health / medical (use with extreme caution — usually outside scope)

**Inline:**
```
General wellness suggestion: increase fiber intake to 25g/day (FDA recommendation)
NOT medical advice. Consult your doctor before changing diet.
```

**Section header:**
```
## Wellness Insights

These suggestions are educational and based on widely-published guidelines
from [FDA / NIH / WHO]. They are NOT medical advice and do not replace
consultation with a licensed healthcare provider.

If you have a medical condition or take prescription medication, consult
your doctor before applying any suggestion below.
```

### §3.5 — Legal (use with extreme caution — usually outside scope)

**Inline:**
```
Suggested clause: [text]
Drafting suggestion only. NOT legal advice. Review with an attorney.
```

**Section header:**
```
## Document Draft

This document is a starting template generated from your inputs.
It is NOT a finished legal document and we are NOT a law firm.

Have an attorney review before signing. Specifically check:
- Jurisdiction-specific clauses
- Liability limits
- Termination provisions
- Indemnification language
```

## §4 — OBBBA-specific disclosure (US fintech 2026)

The One Big Beautiful Bill Act (OBBBA) signed 2025 changed several tax thresholds. Products that touch these must disclose:

- The $500 housing-deduction threshold is OBBBA-derived and applies to filings from 2025 forward
- Section 179 cap changes are reflected in `tax_year_constants` as of [date]
- Multi-year planning views must select the correct tax year — OBBBA rules don't apply retroactively

**Sample OBBBA inline:**
```
Estimated deduction (OBBBA $500 cap applied): $487
Based on documents through [date]. Verify with your CPA.
```

## §5 — AI / LLM disclosure (mandatory when AI is in the loop)

Any product that uses AI to generate user-visible numbers, recommendations, or text MUST disclose:

### §5.1 Inline next to AI-generated content

Either:
- A "✨ AI-generated" badge or icon
- A Provenance component (bookkeeping pack §2 pattern) showing model + prompt_version + confidence
- A user-visible cue (different font weight, italic, color) that distinguishes AI-generated from data-derived

### §5.2 Settings / FAQ explanation

```
## How [Product] uses AI

[Product] uses [Claude / Gemini / OpenAI] for [specific tasks: OCR /
categorization / summarization / drafting]. We do NOT use AI for:
- Final tax filing or financial decisions
- Medical or legal recommendations
- Anything you click "submit" on without reviewing

AI outputs can contain errors. We mitigate this with:
- A [Data Maturity Gate] that hides AI output when source data is sparse
- A [Provenance] component on every AI-generated number
- Logged `prompt_version` for every AI call (so we can audit later)
- A [3-layer fabrication defense] for AI-generated URLs / recommendations

If you find an AI output that's wrong, [link to feedback channel].
```

### §5.3 What NEVER goes to AI APIs

Per `privacy.md` §1, regardless of product:
- SSN, EIN, bank account numbers, routing numbers, full names, full addresses, credit cards, passwords, PHI

The disclosure must explicitly state this. Generic "we use AI safely" copy is not sufficient.

## §6 — TOS-at-signup (every paid product)

Every paid product needs a Terms of Service + Privacy Policy + AI Disclosure linked at signup. The minimum trio:

1. **Terms of Service** — what user agrees to, refund policy, account deletion, dispute resolution
2. **Privacy Policy** — what data is collected, how it's stored, who it's shared with (including AI providers), retention, deletion rights (GDPR 17, CCPA 1798.105)
3. **AI Disclosure** — §5 above, expanded to plain English

These three pages cannot be skipped at signup. Checkbox: "I have read and agree to [Terms], [Privacy], and [AI Disclosure]."

## §7 — Audit log for compliance-sensitive actions

Every regulated product needs an immutable audit log of compliance-sensitive events:

```sql
CREATE TABLE compliance_audit_log (
    id UUID PRIMARY KEY DEFAULT gen_random_uuid(),
    user_id UUID REFERENCES auth.users(id),
    tenant_id UUID,                                    -- if multi-tenant
    event_type TEXT NOT NULL,                          -- e.g., 'tax_export', 'investment_suggestion_shown', 'medical_warning_rendered'
    event_data JSONB,                                  -- inputs that produced the event
    prompt_version TEXT,                               -- if AI was involved
    model_version TEXT,                                -- if AI was involved
    rendered_at TIMESTAMPTZ NOT NULL DEFAULT NOW(),
    user_agent TEXT,
    ip_address INET                                    -- only if jurisdiction requires; otherwise NULL
);

CREATE INDEX idx_compliance_audit_user_time ON compliance_audit_log(user_id, rendered_at DESC);
```

This log is APPEND-ONLY. RLS policy: users can SELECT their own rows, no UPDATE / DELETE for anyone except a service-role-scoped retention job.

## §8 — GDPR / CCPA / state-specific patterns

For products with EU or California users:

### Data subject rights (DSR) endpoints

- **GET /api/account/export** — returns all user data as JSON. Includes audit log entries about the user. Excludes other users' data.
- **DELETE /api/account** — soft-deletes user data with 30-day recovery window, then hard-delete. Cascades through tenant_users, agent_runs, compliance_audit_log (subject to retention requirements).
- Both endpoints rate-limited (1 / 24h per user).

### Consent tracking

Every consent gets a row:

```sql
CREATE TABLE consent_log (
    id UUID PRIMARY KEY DEFAULT gen_random_uuid(),
    user_id UUID NOT NULL REFERENCES auth.users(id),
    consent_type TEXT NOT NULL,                        -- 'tos_v3', 'privacy_v2', 'ai_disclosure_v1', 'marketing_emails'
    consent_version TEXT NOT NULL,                     -- document version hash
    granted BOOLEAN NOT NULL,
    granted_at TIMESTAMPTZ NOT NULL DEFAULT NOW(),
    revoked_at TIMESTAMPTZ,
    UNIQUE (user_id, consent_type, consent_version)
);
```

Re-prompt for consent when document version changes.

## §9 — Verification before launch (compliance checklist)

Every regulated product must pass these checks before public ship:

- [ ] All three layers of disclosure copy (inline + section + settings) on every regulated surface
- [ ] AI disclosure visible at signup (not buried in settings)
- [ ] `compliance_audit_log` table created + populated for every compliance-sensitive event
- [ ] Account deletion endpoint exists + tested (typed "DELETE" confirmation)
- [ ] Data export endpoint returns valid JSON of user's full data
- [ ] Privacy Policy reviewed by attorney within last 12 months
- [ ] Terms of Service reviewed by attorney within last 12 months
- [ ] No PII (SSN/EIN/bank/full-name) sent to AI APIs (verified by prompt log review)
- [ ] `tax_year_constants` populated for current + previous year (if fintech/bookkeeping)
- [ ] OBBBA disclosures present for affected calculations (if US tax product)
- [ ] AI outputs show Provenance / confidence indicator on every user-visible number

## §10 — When to escalate to an attorney

You need an attorney review (not just an AI session) before public launch if:

- The product handles money you don't return (fees, subscriptions, refunds)
- The product makes recommendations users could lose money on
- The product touches health, legal, or regulated professional advice
- The product collects sensitive data subject to state law (CA, NY, IL biometric, etc.)
- The product targets users in regulated industries (healthcare, finance, education)
- The product makes statements about its own accuracy ("99% accurate", "guaranteed")

An attorney review is NOT optional for any product Victor sells through the AI Webmaster retainer tier. Stripe + LLC paperwork + attorney review is the gate to "live" — not a nice-to-have.
